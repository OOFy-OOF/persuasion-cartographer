    About this tool's perspective

    This tool is not neutral. It was built to make persuasion visible, not to decide what is true. No neutrality doesn't mean all claims are equal; it means the choice of which claims to compare is itself a rhetorical act, and this tool says so out loud.

    It does not assess factual accuracy, authorial intent, or moral character. It does not label anything "propaganda." It quotes, names techniques, and explains how they work.

    Abstention is a valid and common result. When the tool cannot ground a claim in an exact quote, it says so.

    Use this map as a starting point for your own reading, not as a conclusion.
